### Why replays fail on new seeds (measured)

- Same two tapes replayed on fresh seeds, weeds ON vs OFF: keiz pair identical (weeds irrelevant); Mater Welon pair seed 1: 75k -> 146k without weeds; other seeds unchanged. Weeds matter only sometimes, and only when they hit a tile the tape needs.
- The dominant factor is the SHOP DRAW. Same tape, no weeds, four seeds: 146k / 78k / 77k / 85k. Shops unlock on days 3,6,...,24 (8 draws with replacement, RNG seeded per day). Each shop instance consumes 1 of each product every 4 turns (single-product shops 2x): a shop = ~6 units/day of demand per product (Yarn 12 wool/day, Pet Cafe 12 carrot/day). Premium goods (milk, wool, strawberry, melon) collapse to $1 without shop demand because their glut curves are linear/quadratic (milk floor at ~76 units over I0, strawberry ~62, wool ~50, melon ~150). Wheat/egg glut curves are log (gentle). Fertilizer has no consumer at all: price = 100 - 0.2 * units sold over I0, straight to 1.
- Market lockstep: each SELL unit is quoted at the current inventory, both players alternate unit by unit, so whoever sells first gets the higher price. Sales at $1 do not add to inventory.
- Bug to avoid in local harnesses: when stepping the env manually, player 1's observation lacks `step` (the framework merges shared keys); an agent that reads obs['step'] then replays step 0 forever.

### Engine facts worth remembering (from kaggriculture.py 1.32.7)

- End of day order per farm: plants refresh (unwatered>=2 -> weed), animals refresh (unfed>=2 -> escape; production; care bonus banked only if fed AND cared), weeds spawn (rng.random() per EMPTY tile, p=0.005), inventories dropped to shed (cap 100, overflow lost), units respawn at shed. Then shop draw uses the same RNG stream, so the shop sequence depends on how many empty tiles both farms had (opponent-dependent, not predictable).
- Seed is a random 31-bit int stored only in the replay (`info.seed`).
- PLANT with more requests than seeds drops ALL plant requests for that crop that turn. New plant starts consecutive_unwatered=1: plant and water the same day or it dies.
- Animal production: cow 1 milk/2 days, sheep 1 wool/3 days, goose 1 egg/day; care adds +1 per fed+cared day paid at next production (cared goose = 2 eggs/day, cared cow = 3 milk/2 days). Each animal also gives 1 fertilizer/day if collected. Products cap at max_held (4 goose, 6 cow/sheep) on the tile until harvested.
- Hire cost fib(n) resets daily; 12 hands = 376/day; a 13th costs 233.

Optimal mix = match (my + opponent's visible) supply to shop demand; the opponent's farm is public in the observation.

Selling policy must be price-impact aware (each unit is quoted at the moving inventory).

## Building an adaptive controller (5 Sep, afternoon) — status and lessons

Files: `experiments/c6.py` (controller), `experiments/c7.py` (lineage tape for the opening, then c6), harnesses `experiments/bench.py`, `dbg.py`, `dbg2.py`, `audit.py`, `endgame.py`, `trace.py`. Pool labels: `v5c`/`v4c` = Codex's immutable anchors, `v5c` self-play scores 82.5k/82.5k on seeds 8801/8803/8807/8819 (both seats).

Executor lessons that cost real money (each verified with `audit.py`, which counts planted/harvested/died per crop from engine state):
- Units carrying produce all day overflow the 100-item shed at the end-of-day auto-drop; overflow is silently discarded. Drop loads at the shed during the day (the tape does), and force the biggest loads home from hour 17 when shed + carried > 100.
- One-time crops: WATER before HARVEST on the harvest day (watering in the bonus window adds a unit instantly). Harvesting first cost a third of every carrot/wheat/melon harvest.
- Never sell the feed wheat: reserve = all owned animals (placed + in shed + planned). Distribute wheat to the units whose regions contain the unfed animals; a farmer alone at hour 0 must not take it all.
- Feeding must outrank routine watering; alternate-day watering makes every plant an "emergency" every other day if the optional watering is skipped, so give the scheduled watering a real priority.
- Nearest-first inside a stable per-day region (angular wedge around the shed) with priority as tie-break beats priority-first scheduling; drop optional work only when the region's remaining actions exceed remaining hours.
- Do not let a projection-driven "review" abandon herds or dig live plants: the projections were wrong often enough to destroy 9 cows and 30 strawberries in one game. Kept off.
- Replant one-time crops on the same tile immediately after harvest (seed buffer bought in the morning); the tape cycles ~25 wheat tiles this way (194 plantings/game) and is feed self-sufficient.

Economics lessons:
- In lineage-vs-lineage games both sides make ~82k because they flood the same premium goods. If one side stops flooding, the other jumps to 90-117k. Margin (my money minus opponent's) is the objective; producing what the opponent produces is often right (spoiler effect), abandoning it is often wrong.
- Feed is the hidden cost: 18 animals x 45-55 per wheat x 18 days = 15k. Growing wheat (log glut, always demanded) is the cheap engine.
- The opponent's ongoing crops are fertilized (2 units per production); model it. Compare the modelled opponent production over the last 3 days with observed market-inventory changes (delta + known consumption - my sales) to detect hoarding.

Current standing (4 seeds x 2 seats vs v5c): c7 hand-over at day 25 = 79.8k vs 81.6k (near parity); hand-over at day 11 = ~78k vs ~88k (still losing). Not submitted; criterion for the first upload is >=60% paired wins against v5c on fresh seeds.

## 7 Sep — adaptive controller `experiments/kc.py` (heavy build, in progress)

Goal: a kwa-class adaptive farm. kwa (#1, 2924; 190 public games, 86% wins) plays the same economy as the lineage but sizes it to demand (24 tomato tiles in a 3-Pizza world, 16 sheep with 2 Yarn stores, 52 strawberry tiles in Smoothie worlds, 9 animals when neither milk nor wool sells), runs 11-12 units from day 6, buys 130-175 feed wheat, sells once each morning. Deterministic with FIXSHOPS.

Engine facts learned the hard way (all verified):
- Hands' inventories are dropped into the shed automatically at the end of every day (capacity 100, overflow discarded). The tape never uses DROP; it keeps the shed nearly empty and lets the auto-drop return produce. Shed reserves (I held 25 wheat + 20 fertilizer) waste that capacity.
- Ongoing crops are credited at the END of a day when (next_day - planted - first) % interval == 0, i.e. strawberry produces at ages 9, 11, 13, 15 and tomato at ages 7-10; the +1 fertilizer bonus needs WATER on that day and fertilizer active. Watering/fertilizing on the "obvious" even ages wastes both (this alone was ~100 strawberries per game). One fertilizer (3 days) covers two strawberry productions when applied on a production day.
- Non-ongoing crops (wheat/carrot/melon) accrue yield only when WATERED on window days (age 2..maxday for wheat: 1 per watering, 2 if fertilized; fertilize at age 2 to cover the whole window). The tape gets 6 wheat per 3.5-day cycle this way.
- Herders must carry their own animals' wheat from the morning shed visit; a shared restock counter starves whoever arrives second.
